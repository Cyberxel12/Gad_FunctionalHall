Terms and Conditions for GAD Function Hall Reservation

  1. Reservation Approval: All reservation requests are subject to approval by the GAD Office or designated administrator. A reservation is confirmed only after receiving an approval notification.
  2. Reservation Schedule: Users must select their preferred date and time. Reservations are granted on a first-approved, first-served basis. Double booking is not allowed.
  3. Authorized Use: The function hall shall be used only for official municipal activities, seminars, meetings, trainings, workshops, and other approved events.
  4. Proper Use of Facility: Users must maintain cleanliness and order before, during, and after the event. Furniture and equipment should be used properly. Report maintenance concerns immediately.
  5. Time Compliance: Users must follow the approved reservation schedule. Extensions require prior approval from the administrator.
  6. Cancellation: Users who wish to cancel their reservation should do so as early as possible to make the schedule available to others.
  7. Administrator's Authority: The administrator may approve, reject, cancel, or reschedule reservations when necessary due to conflicts, emergencies, or official municipal activities.
  8. False Information: Providing inaccurate or misleading information during registration or reservation may result in the rejection or cancellation of the request.
  9. Privacy: Personal information collected during registration and reservation will be used only for reservation management and handled in accordance with applicable data privacy regulations.
  10. Acceptance: By submitting a reservation request, the user acknowledges that they have read, understood, and agreed to these Terms and Conditions.
